Graduate voter enrolment

Your Degree Is Not Your Vote. Enrolment Is.

The Graduates' Constituency has a separate electoral roll. Having a degree alone does not automatically mean your name appears on it — eligible graduates should check the current official enrolment requirements and deadlines and complete the process in time.

Eligibility criteria, required documents and deadlines change with each notification. Please verify the current requirements on the official Telangana Chief Electoral Officer / Election Commission website before applying.

Who can enrol?

Generally, graduates who are ordinarily resident in the constituency and otherwise eligible to vote.

  • Graduate of a recognised university
  • Ordinarily resident in the constituency
  • Otherwise eligible to vote

Confirm the current official eligibility wording before relying on it.

What documents are required?

Typically the following, though the exact list can change with each notification.

  • Degree proof
  • Identity / address proof
  • A recent photograph

Use only the current official document requirements when filing.
