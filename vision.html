Vision for graduates

A Stronger Voice for Graduates

The Hyderabad–Rangareddy–Mahabubnagar Graduates' Constituency represents a diverse community of educated citizens — young professionals, teachers, lecturers, government employees, private-sector employees, entrepreneurs, competitive-exam aspirants, researchers and unemployed graduates.

Their concerns go beyond a degree certificate — employment, skills, recruitment, higher education, opportunities and economic mobility. Gouri Satish's experience in education has shaped six priorities for graduate representation.

01 — Timely Fee Reimbursement

Greater transparency and predictability in fee reimbursement so students receive their certificates on time and institutions can manage their responsibilities.

02 — From Degrees to Skills to Careers

Promote industry-recognised skill modules, certifications, internships and practical learning alongside formal education.

03 — A Year-Round Employment Support System

A constituency-level mechanism covering job notifications, coaching, skill programmes and internships — active all year, not only at election time.

04 — Predictable, Transparent Recruitment

Published recruitment calendars, timely notifications and the filling of sanctioned vacancies in government recruitment.

05 — Regulation That Improves Education

Simpler, more transparent processes for recognition, affiliation and safety clearances — protecting standards, not adding barriers.

06 — Continuity, Classroom to Higher Education

A stronger ecosystem — safe campuses, hostels, transport, rural student support and employability throughout.

A Council seat is an office of voice.

Representation is about accountability, not designation

For Gouri Satish, representation is not simply about holding office. It is about creating a consistent platform through which graduates can raise their concerns, contribute ideas and demand accountability. His experience has been built inside the education system; his aspiration is to take that experience into the Legislative Council.
