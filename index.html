About Gouri Satish

From a farming family to the field of education

Born into a farming family in Nakkalapalli, Moinabad, Gouri Satish worked part-time from the eighth class to support his own education. He graduated from New Government Degree College, Khairatabad, and completed his M.A. and M.Com. at Osmania University.

In 2003 he established his first junior college in Shamshabad. What began as an effort to create an opportunity for himself became a 23-year journey of creating educational opportunities for others.

Education & Public Service

Bridging education, opportunity and representation

For more than two decades Gouri Satish has worked closely with students, parents, educators and institutions — on fee reimbursement, recognition, safety clearances and student welfare. Today he seeks to carry that experience into public representation.

Vision for Graduates

A stronger voice for graduates

The Hyderabad–Rangareddy–Mahabubnagar Graduates' Constituency represents a diverse community of educated citizens. Their concerns go beyond a degree — six priorities shape the vision.

  • Timely fee reimbursement and student support
  • Skills, internships and employability alongside degrees
  • A year-round employment support system
  • Predictable recruitment and transparent examinations
