Building institutions, not just talking about education

Three educational institutions / campuses across Hyderabad and Rangareddy form the foundation of Gouri Satish's education journey. His approach has been built around a simple principle:

A student's economic circumstances should not determine whether they get an opportunity to complete their education.

Creating access for students

Students from trusts, orphanages and economically weaker families have received concessional or free educational opportunities through the institutions. The focus has remained consistent from the first batch:

Access. Affordability. Continuity. Opportunity.

Where students went next

Over the years, students educated through these institutions have gone on to work as teachers, MROs, Group-I and Group-II officers, police personnel, bank employees, private-sector professionals and entrepreneurs. For many, their qualification represented the first graduate achievement in their family.
